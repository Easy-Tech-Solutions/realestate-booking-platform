AGREEMENT OF LEASE

THIS RESIDENTIAL LEASE is entered into this {{ day }} day of {{ month }}, A.D. {{ year }}, by and between {{ landlord_name }}, and {{ tenant_name }} of the City of Monrovia, County of Montserrado, Liberia, hereinafter known and referred to as the “LANDLORD”, {{ landlord_name }}, also of the City of Monrovia, County of Montserrado, Republic of Liberia, hereinafter known and referred to as the “TENANT”, the LANDLORD AND TENANT shall together be called the parties and individually as the party. It is hereby agreed:

THEREFORE, IN CONSIDERATION OF THE PARTIES' COMMON PROMISES AND AGREEMENTS CONTAINED HEREIN, THE TENANT AGREES TO LEASE THE PREMISES FROM THE LANDLORD ON THE FOLLOWING CONDITIONS:

  1. RENT.

    The lease of the property has been agreed by the parties for the value of: {{ rent_display }}, the landlord and tenant are both interested in leasing the property from the landlord on the terms outlined in this personal property lease agreement.

    The Landlord shall receive all benefits of ownership of the property and this agreement shall be recognized as a true lease under the laws of the Republic of Liberia.

  2. Type of Lease.

    This contract will be regarded as a fixed lease. The lease term for the premises is from {{ lease_start }} to {{ lease_end }}.

    At the end of the lease term and no renewal is made, the Tenant may continue to lease the premises under the same terms of this agreement under a month-to-month arrangement.

    The Tenant will pay the rent on or before the first (1st) to fifth (5th) day of each and every month of the term of this lease to the Landlord either by cash, check or mobile money. The Landlord will have the discretion to change the mode of payment and then communicate the said change to the Tenant by written notice or text message.

  3. Late Payments.

    For any rental payment that is not paid within three (3) days of its due date, the Tenant shall pay a late fee of Five United States Dollars ($5.00 USD).

  4. First Rent.

    The Tenant is required to pay the first rent as agreed upon by the parties upon the first (1st) day of the signing of this rental agreement.

  5. Utilities.

    The Tenant will be responsible for paying for all utilities on the premises, including but not limited to electricity, water, cable TV, etc.

  6. Title to Property.

    The Landlord shall retain title to the property. The Tenant only acquires the right to use the property in the regular course of his/her business, but the property shall remain the personal property of the Landlord in fee simple.

  7. Taxes.

    Excluding real property taxes, the Tenant shall be responsible for any or all other taxes associated with using the property during the duration of this lease agreement.

  8. Right of entry (Inspections).

    The Landlord shall have the right to enter the property during the life of this rental agreement at normal working hours, by giving at least twenty-four (24) hours’ notice to the Tenant to conduct inspection; make any necessary repairs, modifications or improvements and provide services as agreed; or for any reasonable purpose. The Landlord shall retain the authority to display property to interested parties, buyers or possible tenants.

  9. Hazardous Materials.

    The Tenant shall not keep on the property any item of a dangerous, flammable or explosive nature that might unreasonably increase the danger of fire or explosion on the property or that might be considered hazardous or extra hazardous by any insurance company.

  10. Rules, Maintenance, and Repairs.

    Maintenance, minor repairs, and servicing of the property, including but not limited to air-conditioning equipment, plumbing fixtures (such as showers, bath tubs, toilets, or sinks), shall be the Tenant’s sole responsibility and expense. The tenant shall be responsible for maintaining the property’s cleanliness and general condition throughout the duration of this lease.

    1. The Tenant shall abide by all laws, ordinances, health and safety standards and the terms of this lease agreement.
    2. Properly dispose of all garbage.
    3. Do not block any structure that is meant to be used for entrance, egress, passage from and to the property.
    4. Keep railings, windows, balconies, and other fittings and structures intact and clean from the exterior view of the building.
    5. Before changing or putting in new transoms, locks, hooks, doorknobs, etc., the tenant must get the Landlord’s approval.
    6. Refrain from acts that cause extreme annoyance to other inhabitants and neighbors; other actions that create intolerably loud noise.
  11. Default.

    The Tenant is entitled to use any legal remedies if the Landlord violates any of the terms and conditions of this rental agreement or any applicable laws, regulations, or codes. The Landlord shall give the Tenant a reasonable amount of time (at least a month) to resolve any breach or failure to comply with any of the terms and conditions of this rental agreement or any relevant laws, rules, or regulations. This time frame will start the day the Tenant receives notice of the violation or failure to comply with the demand to make necessary corrections.

    The Tenant will be in default if they don’t follow the rules, don’t fix the problem, or can’t fix it in a reasonable amount of time. Following a default by the Tenant, the Landlord has the right to end the lease, give the Tenant notice of termination and eviction, and retake possession of the property.

  12. Abandonment.

    In the event that the Tenant abandons the property, the Landlord may declare the Lease terminated, retake possession of the property, enter the premises, remove the Tenant’s belongings, and lease the property to a third party without incurring any liability to the Tenant for doing so. Until the property is leased to another person or is otherwise inhabited by the Landlord or another person under the Landlord’s authority, the Landlord may reclaim from the Tenant any overdue rent in the event that the property is abandoned. The property shall be deemed abandoned in the event the Tenant is physically absent from the property for a period of Forty-Eight (48) days without officially informing the Landlord of the absence.

  13. Modification to document.

    The parties acknowledge and agree that this document constitutes the whole understanding between them and that this Lease may not be amended, modified, changed, or in any manner changed except by a written amendment executed by all parties hereto.

  14. Binding effect.

    The terms, obligations, conditions, and covenants of this Lease shall be binding upon and shall inure to the benefit of the Tenant, the Landlord, their heirs, legal representatives, and successors in interest.

  15. Early Termination.

    The Tenant shall have the right to end this Lease at any time by giving the Landlord written notice at least thirty (30) days in advance of the end of the lease and paying an early termination fee in the amount of Forty United States Dollars (US$40.00). The Tenant shall be liable for rent payments during the notice of termination period.

  16. Compliance with law.

    In relation to the property and the Tenant’s occupancy and use of it, the Tenant agrees to abide by all applicable laws, rules, regulations, codes, and other issuances, including municipal, county, and regulatory bodies' issuances.

  17. Possession.

    By taking possession of the premises, the Tenant recognizes that they have accepted the premises in good order and in their present state. The Tenant has inspected the premises’ condition.

    This agreement may be terminated at the Tenant’s choice if the Landlord fails to provide the Tenant possession of the premises at the beginning of the lease term. The Security Deposit (if any) and any other prepaid rent and fees, including any fees the Tenant paid during the application process prior to the execution of this agreement, shall also be returned to the Tenant in the event the Landlord fails to deliver possession, and the Tenant cancels this agreement.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, THE PARTIES HAVE SET THEIR HANDS AND AFFIXED THEIR SIGNATURES HEREUNTO ON THE DAY AND DATE FIRST ABOVE WRITTEN.
