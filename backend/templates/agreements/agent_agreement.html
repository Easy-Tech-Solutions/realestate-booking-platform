THIS HOME KONET AGENT AGREEMENT is entered into this {{ day }} day of {{ month }}, A.D. {{ year }}, by and between HOME KONET, a property listing and rental platform, of the City of Monrovia, County of Montserrado, Republic of Liberia, hereinafter known and referred to as the “PLATFORM”, and {{ agent_full_name }}, of the City of {{ city }}, County of {{ county }}, Republic of Liberia, hereinafter known and referred to as the “AGENT.”

WHEREAS, the AGENT has voluntarily chosen to register and participate as a Home Konet Agent for the purpose of sourcing available residential properties for consideration and listing on the PLATFORM; and

WHEREAS, the PLATFORM provides a digital property listing and rental service through which property owners, hosts, agents, tenants, and other users may connect in relation to available residential properties; and

WHEREAS, the AGENT understands that the AGENT’s role is limited to sourcing and submitting legitimate properties and the required property information and documentation to the PLATFORM for verification and approval; and

WHEREAS, the AGENT acknowledges that registration as a Home Konet Agent does not make the AGENT an employee, partner, owner, director, or legal representative of the PLATFORM and does not grant the AGENT ownership or legal rights over any property sourced or submitted by the AGENT;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the AGENT’s voluntary registration with the PLATFORM and the mutual understanding of the parties, the AGENT agrees to the following terms and conditions:

Agent Participation

The AGENT voluntarily chooses to register as a Home Konet Agent and agrees to comply with the rules, policies, procedures, verification requirements, and standards established by the PLATFORM.

The AGENT understands that participation is voluntary and that the AGENT shall operate independently in sourcing properties for submission to the PLATFORM.

Role of the Agent

The primary responsibility of the AGENT shall be to identify and source available residential properties that may be suitable for listing on the PLATFORM.

The AGENT may communicate with property owners or their authorized representatives, obtain the necessary property information and supporting documents, collect accurate owner or authorized representative contact information, obtain the correct property location, collect authentic photographs and other relevant listing information, and submit such information to the PLATFORM for verification and approval.

The AGENT understands that merely submitting a property does not guarantee its approval or publication on the PLATFORM. All properties remain subject to the PLATFORM’s verification and approval process.

Agent Not the Property Owner

The AGENT expressly acknowledges that sourcing, submitting, or assisting with the listing of a property does not make the AGENT the owner of that property.

The AGENT shall not represent, imply, or communicate to the PLATFORM, a property owner, prospective tenant, customer, or any other person that the AGENT owns a property merely because the AGENT sourced or submitted the property.

The AGENT shall provide accurate information identifying the actual property owner or authorized representative of every property sourced.

Agent-Owned Property

Where the AGENT personally owns a property or has legitimate legal authority to rent, lease, manage, or otherwise offer a property for occupancy, the AGENT shall not submit such property as a property sourced from another owner.

Any property personally owned or legitimately controlled by the AGENT shall be submitted under the appropriate HOST capacity on the PLATFORM and shall be subject to the PLATFORM’s applicable Host registration, documentation, verification, and approval requirements.

The AGENT shall disclose to the PLATFORM any personal ownership, management interest, or other financial interest in a property being submitted.

Authenticity and Accuracy of Property Information

The AGENT agrees that every property, document, photograph, video, description, location, rental price, owner contact, and other information submitted to the PLATFORM shall be authentic, accurate, truthful, current, and representative of the actual property being submitted.

The AGENT shall not knowingly submit forged, altered, fraudulent, misleading, stolen, or otherwise unauthentic documents.

The AGENT shall not use photographs or videos of one property and present them as photographs or videos of another property. The AGENT shall not knowingly provide an incorrect property location, false rental price, false description, false owner information, or any other misleading information.

Where documents are required for verification, the AGENT shall make reasonable efforts to obtain legitimate and accurate documents from the property owner or authorized representative.

Property Owner Consent

The AGENT shall ensure that the property owner or an authorized representative is aware that the property is being submitted to the PLATFORM.

The AGENT shall not knowingly submit another person’s property without the knowledge or authorization of the property owner or an authorized representative.

The PLATFORM reserves the right to contact the property owner or authorized representative to verify the information submitted by the AGENT.

Verification and Approval

All properties submitted by the AGENT shall be subject to the PLATFORM’s verification and approval process.

The PLATFORM may review property information, ownership documentation, photographs, location, contact information, and any other relevant information before approving or publishing a property.

The PLATFORM reserves the right to reject, suspend, remove, or request additional information concerning any property that does not satisfy its verification requirements.

The AGENT agrees to cooperate with the PLATFORM during the verification process and provide truthful information whenever additional information or clarification is requested.

False, Fraudulent, or Misleading Listings

The AGENT understands that submitting a false, fraudulent, unauthorized, or materially misleading property listing may cause financial loss, legal disputes, reputational damage, or other harm to the PLATFORM, property owners, tenants, prospective tenants, or other persons.

Where the AGENT knowingly or negligently submits an incorrect property, false documents, misleading photographs, inaccurate information, or an unauthorized listing, the PLATFORM may take appropriate action, including removing the property, suspending or terminating the AGENT’s account, withholding applicable compensation, or taking other measures permitted by its policies and applicable law.

Where the actions or negligence of the AGENT result in damage, financial loss, fraud, legal liability, or other harm to the PLATFORM or another person, the AGENT may be held responsible in accordance with the applicable laws of the Republic of Liberia.

Nothing in this Agreement shall prevent the PLATFORM or any affected person from pursuing any lawful right or remedy available under the laws of the Republic of Liberia.

Agent Compensation

For every successful booking completed on a property that was sourced and successfully listed by the AGENT, the AGENT shall receive twenty-five percent (25%) of the commission earned by the PLATFORM from that booking.

The PLATFORM’s current commission on a successful booking is two percent (2%) of the applicable booking value. Accordingly, the AGENT’s current compensation shall be twenty-five percent (25%) of the PLATFORM’s two percent (2%) commission earned from each qualifying successful booking.

For example, where the PLATFORM earns a commission of US $20.00 from a successful booking, the AGENT shall receive twenty-five percent (25%) of that commission, equal to US $5.00.

The AGENT acknowledges that the compensation structure, including the percentage payable to AGENTS and/or the commission charged by the PLATFORM, may be reviewed and changed at the sole discretion of the PLATFORM. Any change to the AGENT compensation structure shall be communicated to the AGENT prior to the change taking effect.

The AGENT shall only be eligible for compensation where the applicable property and booking satisfy the PLATFORM’s requirements. No compensation shall be payable for cancelled, fraudulent, invalid, unauthorized, or otherwise non-qualifying bookings, or where the property was submitted using false, fraudulent, misleading, unauthorized, or materially inaccurate information.

Professional Conduct

The AGENT agrees to conduct themselves honestly, professionally, and respectfully when dealing with property owners, tenants, prospective customers, PLATFORM representatives, and members of the public.

The AGENT shall not engage in fraudulent, deceptive, abusive, threatening, or unlawful conduct in connection with their activities on or through the PLATFORM.

Confidentiality and Protection of Information

The AGENT agrees to protect confidential information obtained through their activities with the PLATFORM.

Such information may include property owner contact information, identification documents, property documents, customer information, internal PLATFORM information, and other information that is not publicly available.

The AGENT shall not sell, distribute, disclose, misuse, or otherwise share such information with unauthorized persons or use such information for purposes unrelated to their legitimate activities with the PLATFORM.

Independent Participation

The AGENT understands that registration as a Home Konet Agent does not make the AGENT an employee, partner, director, owner, or legal representative of the PLATFORM.

The AGENT participates independently and is not authorized to make commitments, agreements, representations, or decisions on behalf of the PLATFORM unless expressly authorized by the PLATFORM in writing.

Suspension or Termination

The PLATFORM may suspend or terminate the AGENT’s participation where the AGENT violates this Agreement, repeatedly submits properties that fail verification, provides false or fraudulent information, misrepresents property ownership, submits unauthorized properties, engages in misconduct, or otherwise acts in a manner that may expose the PLATFORM or its users to unnecessary risk or liability.

Termination or suspension of the AGENT’s participation shall not prevent the PLATFORM or any affected party from pursuing any lawful rights or remedies arising from the AGENT’s conduct.

Acknowledgement of Responsibility

By signing this Agreement and/or completing the Home Konet Agent registration process, the AGENT confirms that they have read, understood, and agreed to the terms and conditions contained herein.

The AGENT acknowledges that their primary responsibility is to source and submit legitimate properties belonging to other property owners or authorized representatives. The AGENT further acknowledges that they are not the owner of a property merely because they sourced or submitted the property to the PLATFORM.

The AGENT agrees that any property they personally own or are legally authorized to manage shall be submitted under the appropriate HOST capacity rather than being represented as a property sourced from another owner. The AGENT also acknowledges their responsibility to ensure that all documents, photographs, property information, owner information, and other materials submitted to the PLATFORM are authentic, accurate, and related to the actual property being submitted.

Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and interpreted in accordance with the applicable laws of the Republic of Liberia. Any dispute arising from or relating to this Agreement shall be addressed in accordance with the applicable laws and legal procedures of the Republic of Liberia.
